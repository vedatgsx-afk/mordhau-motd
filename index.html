🛡️ Server Rules & Guidelines

General Rules

  1. No FFA (Free For All): Do not FFA, revenge FFA, or mass FFA. Friendly FFA is allowed as long as it does not interfere with active duels, the center, or other players.
  2. Respect Duels: Do not interrupt ongoing duels or the Arena. Always ask for a duel properly before fighting.
  3. No Harassment & Slurs: Zero tolerance for racism, homophobia, xenophobia, or any discriminatory slurs.
  4. Cheating & Glitches: Using any form of hack or third-party cheat will result in a permanent ban.
  5. TP & Healing Restrictions: Do not use heals or teleportation while actively dueling. Do not block, trap, or fight near TP spots.
  6. No Exploitative Gear: Do not use bear traps, firebombs, or smoke bombs in CC.
  7. Building & Ballistas: No building unless authorized by an admin. Ballistas and building structures are restricted to designated side areas.
